💰 Personal Finance App

Manage your budget and track your investment portfolio in one place.

📖 Description

A personal finance application that lets you record income and expenses, organize a monthly budget, and track the performance of an investment portfolio (indexes such as the S&P 500, gold, cryptocurrencies, etc.), without relying on spreadsheets.

✨ Features
📊 Portfolio tracking: add your assets and check their value and performance.
🧾 Monthly budget: record income and expenses by category.
📈 Visual overview: see at a glance where your money goes and how your net worth is growing.
🖥️ Installable version: download and install the app in a few steps.
🚀 Installation
Go to the Releases section of this repository.
Download the installer for the latest version.
Run it and follow the setup wizard.
Open the app and start using it.
🧭 Getting Started
Create your first monthly budget.
Add your assets to the portfolio, entering quantity and purchase price.
Record your income and expenses.
Check the main dashboard for a summary of your situation.
